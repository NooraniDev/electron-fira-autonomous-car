## National Competition — Second Place, Iran

Competing in the FIRA Autonomous Mobile Robotics category at the Iranian national competition, **ELECTRON** achieved **Second Place**. The platform was evaluated on autonomous navigation, visual marker and object recognition, and reliable execution under competition conditions.

## International Stage — Qualified for FIRA International Competition

Through its Second Place national finish, **ELECTRON** earned official qualification to compete at the **FIRA International Competition**.
