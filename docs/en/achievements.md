## Official Competition Results

The **ELECTRON** autonomous mobile robot was developed for the **FIRA Robotics Competition** and achieved the following verified results:

| Level | Achievement | Competition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **National (Iran)** | **Second Place (2nd Place)** | Iran National FIRA Robotics Competition |
| **International** | **Qualified** | FIRA International Competition |

---

## National Achievement — Second Place, Iran

Competing against robotics teams at the Iranian national FIRA competition, **ELECTRON** earned **Second Place** in autonomous mobile robotics. The robot was evaluated on its ability to perceive its environment, interpret visual navigation cues, and execute autonomous maneuvers reliably under competition constraints.

## International Qualification — FIRA International Competition

On the strength of its Second Place national finish, **ELECTRON** **qualified to compete in the FIRA International Competition**.

> **Accuracy Note:** This document records the team's qualification for the FIRA International Competition. Additional details such as competition dates, exact league designation, and future international outcomes are marked as _Pending verification_ until officially confirmed by the team.
