### 1. Purpose of This Repository

This repository is the **public engineering portfolio and showcase** for the **ELECTRON** autonomous mobile robotics project developed for the **FIRA Robotics Competition** (Second Place — Iran National FIRA Competition; Qualified for the FIRA International Competition).

The robot's competition source code, computer-vision pipelines, trained models, control logic, calibration data, electrical schematics, and competition strategies are **confidential and proprietary** to the ELECTRON team.
